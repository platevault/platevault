## Detailed finding adjudications

### PROD-01 — ACCEPT: Review archive disposal before executing it

ACCEPT, P1. ArchivePage.tsx:124-127 immediately calls the trash mutation and :192-196 submits permanent deletion; archive/store.ts:39-48 calls the disposal IPC directly. crates/app/core/src/plans/archive.rs:124-198 and :215-314 enumerate archive_path values from the earlier plan and execute trash/delete without generating or approving the new action. This contradicts README.md:8-9,35-39 and the explicit archive rule in specs/030-ui-audit-revision/spec.md:333-335. Counterevidence inspected: permanent deletion requires exact DELETE and honors block_permanent_delete (:223-242); path resolution rejects escapes/unresolved roots (:35-59), with corresponding tests at :344-378; missing files are skipped. These are real safeguards, but approving the original relocation does not review subsequent disposal. This finding does not establish arbitrary-path deletion or absence of every confirmation. Remaining verification: disposable archive fixture, cancellation/no mutation before approval, exact affected-item review, and policy refusal through direct IPC.

Source: `apps/desktop/src/features/archive/ArchivePage.tsx:124-127`.

### PROD-02 — ACCEPT: Preserve mixed archive-disposal failures

ACCEPT, P1. In crates/app/core/src/plans/archive.rs:147-183, failed trash operations only update last_failure and log a warning; an error is returned only when items_moved is zero. The corresponding delete loop and condition at :261-299 behave identically. The events at :193 and :309 contain only plan ID, successful count, and timestamp, confirmed by crates/audit-types/src/event_bus.rs:326-340. Thus one success suppresses the structured failure even when other archived files remain, and the page ignores the returned success count as well (archive/store.ts:117-140). Counterevidence: the counts represent actual successes; all-failed attempts produce errors; repeated calls intentionally skip already-absent files. The common plan executor has partial-outcome regression assertions in crates/app/core/tests/plan_apply_lifecycle_integration.rs:119-168, but the direct archive path bypasses it. No claim is made that external tracing cannot retain warnings; the demonstrated issue is loss from the command result and emitted archive-event payload. Remaining verification: mixed OS/permission/path failures, restart-visible audit, all-failure and repeated-disposal scenarios, with remaining failed items identified individually.

Source: `crates/app/core/src/plans/archive.rs:176-183`.

Remaining verification: two interrupted plans, successful resume, partial resume then successful retry, discard, and close/reopen; preserve original queue identity separately from retry identity.

Source: `apps/desktop/src/features/recovery/RecoveryBanner.tsx:68-77`.
