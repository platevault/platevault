# Library data model

## Durable entities

- Location: UUID, display name, native root path, role, decision revision, recorded volume/filesystem identity and root file identity, last successful observation, access/availability and lifecycle Active or Retired. Registration is read-only and rejects same/ancestor/descendant roots of Active locations on the same volume. Case/Unicode display variations do not create a second root identity.
- Asset: UUID, location ID, lossless relative path, observation size/nanosecond mtime and qualified stable file identity where supported. VolumeIdentity is filesystem type plus remount-stable UUID/serial, never mount path or device number alone. Unknown/unstable file IDs are excluded from decision fingerprints and deduplication; path plus size/mtime remains evidence and path adoption requires byte proof. Inode reuse or an ID match with changed size/mtime does not adopt a renamed path.
- Observation: asset ID, original header values, extraction outcome/error, scan ID and sequence. Unknown values remain null; zero is never a missing-value substitute. Effective catalog corrections are separate from this original evidence.
- Correction: per-asset field/value, independent decision revision and manual provenance. Preview names expected per-record decision revisions and current observation fingerprints. Confirmation atomically commits corrections and recomputed grouping; stale input refuses the whole batch.
- Session: UUID, deterministic CaptureKey, grouping revision, exact asset IDs, unresolved evidence and observed date basis. OBJECT/Target are outside identity. A current session ID stays stable while identical capture evidence adds assets; grouping changes preserve the predecessor record.
- SessionLineage: correction ID, predecessor session IDs, successor session IDs, grouping revision and moved asset IDs. Fixed View memberships continue referencing asset IDs and their recorded revisions.
- Association: session ID, kind Target/Equipment, Unresolved/NeedsReview/Suggested/Confirmed, evidence items, provenance, decision revision and observation basis. Rescans preserve confirmed/manual associations. Coverage counts Confirmed or evidence-qualified Suggested associations and labels their provenance; unresolved/conflicting candidates do not count. A regroup successor inherits a confirmation only if all its assets share it, otherwise NeedsReview.
- Target: UUID, designation/normalized aliases, coordinates/frame, provider/seed/user provenance and decision revision. Catalog owns storage; targets.rs owns shared-library normalization and pure candidate/provider logic. Saving/adopting is explicit.
- Equipment: UUID, camera/optical-train evidence, observed/confirmed state and decision revision. Confirmation does not alter capture grouping without a separately reviewed grouping correction.
- Quality: asset ID, explicit decision/revision and reviewed-content SHA-256 plus observation fingerprint. Review hashes bytes; readable scans/reuse reverify decided assets even when stats match. Until that rehash finishes, the decision reads verification pending, stays outside applicable Usable totals and keeps the scan scope provisional; a canceled or failed rehash leaves it pending. A digest mismatch or absent historical review basis makes quality ChangedContent until reconfirmed or a later rehash matches the reviewed digest again. Unreadable/offline inputs retain their prior digest and last-observed quality, with unavailable/unverified scope, not an invented content change.
- Digest evidence: optional SHA-256 with its observation basis. New Unreviewed indexing stays metadata-only; quality review, verification of previously decided or aliased assets, cross-location duplicate-candidate verification, remap and custody work hash actual bytes lazily. Equal size/mtime never substitutes for content proof. Offline remap without valid prior digest refuses with NoByteProof.
- Scan operation: UUID, location ID, monotonic committed revision, Running/Completed/Partial/Failed/Canceled, counters, complete/incomplete scopes and item errors. Activity lists persisted operations after restart, even without a remembered operationId.

## Location and absence proof

Before every batch and final reconciliation, validate the registered stable volume identifier and root file identity. A session-local device number alone does not prove remount identity. Missing/unqualified evidence, a readable empty replacement directory, disconnection or a different volume is Offline/IdentityConflict; no assets are created/reused and none become Missing. Absence applies only within positively complete scopes on the same identity, excluding unreadable/skipped-link scopes.
Nested directories on a different volume are boundary scopes: skip traversal and preserve their exclusion from absence reconciliation. Revalidate root/volume identity immediately before applying each batch and inside the final reconciliation transaction. A mid-scan mismatch downgrades scope to incomplete and prevents Missing updates.

Retirement is the only exit from a location whose identity cannot be re-verified. A reviewed retire commits lifecycle Retired with the location's last-observed state and changes no file. Retired is not Offline: it removes the root from overlap checks and refuses reselect, scan and remap, and its assets read Retired, never Missing. View memberships and prepared revisions keep their asset IDs and name retired copies unresolved. Registering the same folder again creates a new location and new asset identities; retired decisions stay as history and never transfer automatically.

Registration compares canonical filesystem identity and ancestry without universal case folding. Within a qualified volume, path equality follows its case/normalization behavior and requires matching size/nanosecond mtime. General inode-only rename adoption is not supported; new paths need reviewed byte proof. Unknown identity refuses identity-dependent mutations rather than guessing.

Remap reviews are durable catalog plans with original/candidate identities, SHA-256 evidence and expected revisions; review writes no image files. Apply is all-or-nothing for a location. Every asset must pass current byte and identity checks before the root changes. A refused candidate or NoByteProof leaves all asset paths, decisions and original location unchanged. Lazy prior digest evidence must match its observation fingerprint; readable originals/candidates are rehashed at apply.

## CaptureKey

Canonical CaptureKey values include frame type, header-derived night/date basis, camera, optical train, filter, exposure, gain, offset, binning, dimensions, readout and cooler setpoint. Finite decimals use parsed numeric values with canonical round-trip encoding, normalizing negative zero; invalid values retain raw evidence and are unknown in keys. Measured temperature, mechanical-rotation jitter and pointing remain diagnostics, not exact identity fields. Intentional equipment/rotator changes require a reviewed grouping correction.

Night derives only from header DATE-LOC with a noon boundary, or capture time plus recorded longitude with an explicitly labelled mean-solar noon boundary. Without either, use labelled UTC date with provisional night evidence. No wall-clock or app-site/equipment-setting change alters capture identity. Corrections create reviewed grouping lineage.

## Atomicity and durability

One serialized writer owns the SQLite connection; readers use separate connections. Every writer connection uses WAL, foreign keys and FULL synchronous mode, with fullfsync/checkpoint_fullfsync on macOS. Scan batches are short. Corrections, their regroup and lineage commit in one transaction using current observed evidence. Decision revisions are independent of scan observation sequences.

Multi-record commands use `{id, expectedDecisionRevision, expectedObservationFingerprint}` lists and apply all or nothing. Unchanged rescans do not invalidate decision CAS. Restart restores committed records and marks interrupted scans incomplete. A disposable SQLite `max_page_count` fixture forces SQLITE_FULL to prove PersistenceFailure and no saved acknowledgment; no failure switch is compiled into release and no custom userspace WAL is introduced.

## Progress

Captured integration sums effective corrected-or-observed exposure for known light assets of Active locations, including offline last-observed contributions. Usable counts only applicable fingerprint-bound library decisions. Unreviewed, drifted decisions and unknown exposure counts remain visible separately. Scope identifies covered location IDs and provisional/complete state. Calibration assets never inflate light integration.

Retired assets stay listed as history and count toward no captured, usable or Unreviewed integration. This deliberately differs from Offline (LIB-FR-07/08), because a retired location never returns through reselect. Re-registering its folder indexes the same captures as new metadata-only assets, and D16 aliases only hashed copies, so counting both would double count. D19 also leaves no basis to re-verify.

Assets in different registered locations with equal SHA-256 are one logical capture. Totals count it once; sessions, View selection and refresh list one member that names every physical copy. An applicable decision on any copy applies to the logical capture, and conflicting explicit decisions count as neither Usable nor Unreviewed. Unhashed assets with equal size, CaptureKey and capture start time are duplicate candidates, hashed before totals leave provisional scope. Readable rescans rehash aliased copies under D19. Candidates or aliased copies whose SHA-256 differ are conflicting copies: the capture counts once, labelled conflicting, and neither copy substitutes for the other until reviewed. Every copy stays registered and protected; aliasing authorizes no disposal (D16).
