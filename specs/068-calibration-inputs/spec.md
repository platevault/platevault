## User Scenarios & Testing

### User Story 1 - Calibration matching at ingestion, the calibration library, and master adoption (Priority: P1)

Calibration is matched when sessions come in. At ingestion, flats, darks and bias are matched to each light session under the rules below and pinned on the session; a match that misses a check or falls outside its window is only suggested and needs confirmation. A Project looks up its sessions' calibration, and its Calibrate step is a read-only check with a WBPP-style calibration diagram. The Calibration library imports master files after checking their headers, keeps each master's dated versions, flags old darks and bias, hides masters the user no longer wants offered, and offers a generated master found in a Project's `Processing/` folder once for adoption (D-W82, D-W98, D-W101 to D-W106).

**Why this priority**: This feature owns the workflow steps listed below without imposing unrelated product features.

**Independent Test**: Exercise the acceptance scenarios through the running product with isolated inputs. Existing baseline code does not prove the redesigned behavior.

**Acceptance Scenarios**:

(D-W101, D-W102, D-W103)

The session waits in "Needs calibration", and no Project can add it until the user confirms those flats or chooses others. (D-W102, D-W105)

- **CAL-AC-05**: Given explicit adoption, then the master appears in Calibration with its origin and provenance and can be pinned automatically where every criterion is compatible. (D-W5)
- **CAL-AC-06**: Given a session whose best flat has an unknown rotation on a rig with a rotator, when it is ingested, then the flat is offered as a suggestion, and the session waits in "Needs calibration" until the user confirms it or chooses another. (D-W102, D-W105)
- **CAL-AC-07**: Given a generated master in a processing folder, when adoption is confirmed, then its copy is durably written and re-read/hash verified at the chosen calibration-library destination before reuse is registered. Failed verification retains the candidate and no reusable record is claimed.
- **CAL-AC-08**: Given adoption is reviewed and an unrelated file then appears at the master's destination path, when adoption is confirmed, then it is refused for that path and names the existing file. That file stays byte-identical, no copy is written, nothing is registered and the generated source remains. A free path then adopts under CAL-AC-07. Given instead that the entry at the destination is PlateVault's own unregistered copy, left there by a registration that source drift blocked (CAL-AC-09). When adoption is confirmed again, it is refused, and the refusal names that copy as PlateVault's own and offers Move to Trash for it. After the user reviews and confirms the move, the copy is in the OS Trash, and adoption retries: it copies, verifies and registers the master at that destination. The copy is never overwritten, reused or registered. (D-W119)
- **CAL-AC-09**: Given an adoption review recorded the candidate's identity and SHA-256, when the source bytes change before confirmation, or after the copy verifies but before registration, then adoption is blocked with source drift named. Nothing is registered or suggested, and no copy is offered for reuse; any copy already written is named and stays unregistered. A new review of the current bytes is required before adoption, and an adoption to the destination that copy occupies follows CAL-AC-08. (D-W119)
- **CAL-AC-10**: Given an adopted master's library copy replaced in place with its size and mtime preserved, when matching runs for a new session, then the master reads drifted against its adoption digest. It is neither pinned nor suggested, and it stays protected with its adoption provenance as history. Restoring the adopted bytes restores automatic pinning with no new adoption. (D-W5)

After Hide, it is not offered again for that file and digest, and it stays listed as a candidate in Calibration, where it can still be adopted. (D-W5, D-W55, D-W98)

Nothing is imported until the user confirms, and the Duplicate is not imported. (D-W82, D-W103)

The library holds dark master D whose newest version is 120 days old, a flat made 200 days ago and bias B whose newest version is 30 days old. Hide on a master that sessions of an open or finished (Closed or Archived) Project use is refused, and the refusal names those Projects. This refusal is provisional. (D-W76, D-W82, D-W106)
- **CAL-AC-19**: Given three new sessions from a camera with no bias in the library, when the user selects them in "Needs calibration" and chooses No bias, then they read No bias with no reason asked, and they leave "Needs calibration" once their darks and flats are settled. (D-W105)
- **CAL-AC-20**: Given library master D, pinned only on sessions that no Project holds, and library master B, pinned on a session that an open Project holds. When the user chooses Move to Trash on D, then its confirmation lists the sessions D is pinned on, which then need calibration again; after confirming, D's files go to the OS Trash and D leaves the library. Move to Trash on B is refused, and the refusal names that Project. This refusal is provisional. (D-W82, D-W106)
- **CAL-AC-21**: Given a session with raw flats ingested with its lights and a master flat that matches it, when the View is built, then it gets the master flat. When the user switches the session to its raw flats, the next Apply places the raw flats instead. (D-W102)
- **CAL-AC-22**: Withdrawn (D-W103). There is no Latest version and no Update notice; CAL-AC-28 covers the Calibrate step's diagram.
- **CAL-AC-23**: Given a session captured on 1 Oct 2026 and dark versions from 10 Sep 2025 and 20 Sep 2026 with the default 365-day window, then the 20 Sep 2026 version is pinned. When only the 10 Sep 2025 version exists, it is suggested with both capture dates shown and needs confirmation, because it is 386 days older than the lights. (D-W103, D-W114)
- **CAL-AC-24**: Given the flat window set to 30 nights for a fixed setup, when a session is ingested 12 nights after the last flats on the same rig version, then those flats are pinned automatically. With the default window they are suggested. (D-W102)
- **CAL-AC-25**: Given an import that holds dark flats, then they are never matched, suggested or assigned to a session, and no View contains them. (D-W93)
- **CAL-AC-26**: Given flats whose ROTATANG differs from the lights by 1°, 4° and 7° on a rig with a rotator, then the 1° set can be pinned, the 4° set is suggested with a warning, and the 7° set isn't offered. (D-W102, D-W114)
- **CAL-AC-27**: Given a Project whose 2024 session is pinned to the 2024 darks and whose 2026 session is pinned to the 2026 darks with the same settings, when the Calibrate step opens, then the diagram shows each night with its own dark version and marks the mix. The Apply sheet places each dark set in the night folders of the lights that use it. (D-W99, D-W101)
- **CAL-AC-28**: Given a Project with two groups over three nights, when the Calibrate step opens, then the diagram shows each group's nights with the flats, darks and bias they get, each with its version and date, and it offers no assignment control. A night whose session is set to No flats reads No flats. (D-W101)
- **CAL-AC-29**: Given a session pinned to master flat F, when F's library file changes in place or goes missing, then the pin stays and the session reads F as changed or missing, with Reassign…. "Needs calibration" lists the session. The session remains settled for Project membership and may be added to a Project while the pin is drifted. The next Apply of a Project that holds the session is refused with a link to reassign F, and no other flat is pinned by itself. Restoring F's bytes takes the session out of "Needs calibration" and lets Apply proceed. (D-W113; owner's answer, 2026-10-19, astro-plan-rzbzu.1)
- **CAL-AC-30**: Given a bias master B and a session with 300 s lights, both at gain 100 and offset 50, then B is pinned although no bias has a 300 s exposure. For darks the exposure still counts: only a 300 s dark master can be pinned to that session. The bias checks this relies on are provisional (CAL-FR-21). (D-W103, D-W114)
- **CAL-AC-31**: Given one night that intake split into a session at ROTATANG 10° and a "framing 2" session at 95°, with flats taken at 10°. Then the flats are pinned on the first session and aren't offered to the second. (D-W102, D-W107)
- **CAL-AC-32**: Given Settings, when the user opens Matching in its Library group, then `/settings/matching` shows one table of Setting, Value and Unit, grouped Framing, Flats, Darks and Bias. It holds the defaults of D-W114, such as rotation within 5° under Framing and 365 days under Darks, and Settings > Equipment shows no thresholds. After Edit… sets the flat rotation warning limit to 4°, the table reads 4°, and the next ingestion matches flats with it. (D-W114, D-W148)

### Edge Cases

The [root contract](../063-clean-rebuild-contract/spec.md) governs file custody, uncertainty, exact membership, failed writes, and independent lifecycle decisions. Each acceptance scenario tests observable outcomes; no mock acknowledgment counts as terminal success.

- When more than one input is fully compatible, the top-ranked one is pinned automatically and the others are listed as alternatives. A ranking tie that leaves no single top input is offered as a suggestion. (D-W5, D-W102)
- A pinned assignment the user reassigns stays as the user chose: later matching never overrides it. (D-W106)
- A generated master in `Processing/` that is hidden and later changes content is a new candidate and is offered once again. (D-W5, D-W98)

## Requirements

### Functional Requirements

- **CAL-FR-01**: The Calibration surface lists masters and raw calibration sets (flats, darks and bias) from indexed locations, grouped by camera, settings, channel, and relevant geometry, with compatibility and missing evidence. It includes the calibration frames Import routes to a Calibration location (LIB-FR-16, STO-IMP-FR-02). Dark flats are ignored: never matched, suggested or assigned. (D-W24, D-W82, D-W93)
- **CAL-FR-02**: Matching runs at ingestion for each new light session (LIB-FR-23), and again for sessions still waiting when calibration is imported later (LIB-FR-25). Each kind, darks, flats and bias, gets its best match under CAL-FR-19 to CAL-FR-23. A match that passes every check inside its window is pinned on the session automatically and recorded under CAL-FR-08; any other match stays a suggestion that needs confirmation. Matching never replaces a pinned assignment; only the user's Reassign… does (LIB-FR-25). A Project never matches calibration itself. (D-W101 to D-W106)
- **CAL-FR-03**: Why this match lists compatible, incompatible and unknown criteria for every pinned and suggested assignment, with the dates of both sides, and remains available after pinning. (D-W5, D-W103)
- **CAL-FR-04**: Raw flats are ingested with the lights. Master flats are imported like darks and bias (Import masters, CAL-FR-13) or adopted from a generated master (CAL-FR-06). When a session has both, the View gets the master flat, and the user can switch a session to its raw flats. Raw dark and bias sets can be pinned as well, for an application that builds its own masters. For every kind, when a master and a raw set both match, the master is preferred. (D-W82, D-W102, D-W114)
- **CAL-FR-05**: For each kind on a session (LIB-FR-23), the user can confirm a suggestion, pick another input, choose No darks, No flats or No bias, which needs no reason, or leave it for later. Confirming a suggestion with an unknown criterion records that criterion with the confirmation. A choice never rewrites master evidence or makes a master universally compatible. (D-W5, D-W82, D-W105)
- **CAL-FR-06**: Detected candidate masters show Add to calibration library, with type, camera/settings, channel, source evidence, and origin. Provisional: a generated master that PlateVault newly finds in a Project's `Processing/` folder is offered once, on the Project's Output step, alongside Import masters in the library (CAL-FR-13). Hide records the decline for that file and digest, so the offer does not return; the candidate stays listed in Calibration. Adoption needs explicit confirmation and records actual provenance. Detection alone never makes a master available for reuse. Candidate and adopted masters are protected by default (STO consumes this). (D-W5, D-W55, D-W82, D-W98)
- **CAL-FR-07**: Adopted master storage follows D05 in the root decision register. Adoption never leaves a reusable master disposable along with its processing folder. Adoption review records the candidate's identity and SHA-256, and the evidence shown is bound to that digest. Copying hashes the bytes it reads and compares them with the reviewed digest; the destination re-read must match it, and immediately before registration the source's current identity and digest must still match. Any mismatch blocks adoption and registers nothing. Adoption checks the destination path during review and again immediately before writing, and an existing entry there is never overwritten. The entry may be PlateVault's own unregistered copy, such as the verified copy that a registration blocked by source drift left there; PlateVault knows it by its recorded operation entry and digest. Adoption then refuses and names the copy, and the refusal offers a reviewed Move to Trash for it under the custody rules of STO-FR-04 and STO-FR-05. Once the copy is in the OS Trash, adoption retries with every check above run again. The copy is never reused or registered. Any other entry at the destination blocks adoption until another path or destination is chosen. The generated source retained after verified adoption remains protected Keep until separately reviewed as a verified duplicate. (D-W119)
- **CAL-FR-08**: Every pinned assignment records the selected calibration identities, for a master its version, their SHA-256 and evidence (D19). An adopted master is pinned or suggested only while its current bytes match its adoption digest; on drift it reads drifted, stays protected and needs review. Apply re-verifies every assignment it places (PREP-FR-09). When a pinned file changes or goes missing, the pin stays, and nothing switches to another file by itself. The session shows the file as changed or missing and waits in "Needs calibration" (LIB-FR-17, LIB-FR-23), and Apply refuses with a link to reassign it on the session (VSEL-FR-14). The drifted pin remains settled for Project membership, so the session may be added while it waits; Apply waits until the pin is reassigned or its recorded bytes return (D-W113; owner's answer, 2026-10-19, astro-plan-rzbzu.1). An unconfirmed suggestion never enters a View, and a session with one is not settled (LIB-FR-23). (D-W5, D-W101, D-W105, D-W113)
- **CAL-FR-09**: A Project's Calibrate step is a read-only check. A WBPP-style calibration diagram shows each group's nights with the flats, darks and bias they get, each with its version and date, or its No choice. It marks a night that mixes versions with the same settings, which the View layout places per night (PREP-FR-16). The step offers no assignment and no Proceed gate: Reassign… on a session (LIB-FR-25) is the way to change one, and it reaches the Project as a pending change. Calibration doesn't need to be the same across a Project. Only settled sessions join a Project (PRJ-FR-09), so the check has no unresolved row. (D-W101, D-W106)
- **CAL-FR-10**: Withdrawn (D-W101). Matching is per session at ingestion: darks and bias match the session's camera (CAL-FR-21), and flats its rig version (CAL-FR-19).
- **CAL-FR-11**: Withdrawn (D-W94, D-W101). There are no run groups; the sessions of each panel group carry their own pinned calibration.
- **CAL-FR-12**: Withdrawn (D-W105). A session joins a Project only once its calibration is settled, so there is no missing-calibration warning; exposure mismatch comes from the Project's sessions (PRJ-FR-11).
- **CAL-FR-13**: Import masters in the Calibration library lets the user pick master files through the native file dialog, with several files at once and FITS and XISF filters. A preview table then shows each file with its kind, camera, gain and offset, temperature and exposure read from its headers, and its match: **New**, **New version of {master}** when its kind and settings match an existing library master, or **Duplicate** when its bytes match a library master (provisional copy). A file whose headers do not identify it as a master is named and not imported. When the rigs hold two or more cameras of the file's model, the preview asks which camera alias it belongs to (LIB-FR-26). Confirming imports the selected files into a Calibration location under the Import custody rules (STO-IMP-FR-03 to STO-IMP-FR-05); a Duplicate is skipped. A new version follows CAL-FR-14. (D-W82, D-W103, D-W104)
- **CAL-FR-14**: A library master is one record with an ordered list of dated **versions**. The record holds its kind, its camera (or camera alias) and the settings it matches; each version is one file with its SHA-256, its adoption provenance and its capture date. No version is marked Latest: matching pins the version taken closest to the lights (CAL-FR-21), and a pinned version changes only when the user reassigns it. Add version… on a master (provisional copy), and an Import masters file that matches it (CAL-FR-13), use the same file choice and matching step and add a version. Adding a version moves no session, so there is no Update notice and no bulk update. (D-W82, D-W103)
- **CAL-FR-15**: A dark or bias master whose newest version is older than N days carries an "Old · {n} d" badge in the Calibration library, as a reminder to shoot new ones, where N is a setting that defaults to 90; flats are exempt. The badge raises no issue on any other row. (D-W82, D-W103)
- **CAL-FR-16**: Hide acts on the whole master with all its versions. It removes the master from the Calibration list, matching and offers without touching its file; Show hidden lists hidden masters with Restore. Provisional: Hide is refused while an open or finished (Closed or Archived) Project uses the master through its sessions, and the refusal names those Projects. Otherwise its confirmation lists the sessions the master is pinned on, which then need calibration again. Hide on an Output-step offer is the decline of CAL-FR-06. (D-W76, D-W82, D-W106)
- **CAL-FR-17**: **Move to Trash** sits beside Hide and acts on the whole master: it moves every version's file to the OS Trash and removes the master. Its review names each file with its identity and SHA-256. Provisional: it is refused while an open or finished (Closed or Archived) Project uses the master through its sessions, and the refusal names those Projects; otherwise its confirmation lists the sessions it is pinned on, which then need calibration again. PV-STO executes the move under the custody rules of STO-FR-04 and STO-FR-05: execution re-verifies the file immediately before the move (D19), files go to the OS Trash only with no permanent delete, and a location without OS Trash refuses the move and leaves the file in place. (D-W82, D-W106)
- **CAL-FR-18**: Withdrawn (D-W103). There is no Latest version, no Update notice and no bulk update.
- **CAL-FR-19**: A flat matches a light session only when it passes the hard checks: camera, optical train, filter, binning, sensor size and the same rig version as the lights (LIB-FR-26). Any rig version change, including a camera remount, makes earlier flats incompatible; this rule applies to flats only. A flat that fails a hard check is never offered. (D-W102, D-W104)
- **CAL-FR-20**: Flats are matched per session, and each session has one rotation, because intake splits sessions by framing (LIB-FR-04). A flat's rotation comes from ROTATANG. Thresholds, adjustable in Settings: within 2° is fine, 2° to 5° gives a warning, over 5° isn't offered, and a missing angle on a rig with a rotator counts as unknown. Age counts in nights between flats and lights, in either direction; the window is a setting whose default is the same or the next night, and a fixed setup can use 30 or 60. A flat is pinned automatically only when every check passes, the rotation is known (or the rig has no rotator) and it falls inside the window. Otherwise it is suggested with its date and evidence and needs confirmation. Provisional ranking: same night first, then fewest nights apart, then the smallest rotation difference. (D-W102, D-W107, D-W114)
- **CAL-FR-21**: Darks and bias are matched against the session's camera, by alias where the rigs hold several cameras of its model (LIB-FR-26), and against its capture time, because a dark records the sensor's state when it was taken. The version pinned is the one taken closest to the lights, before or after. The age window for darks and bias is a setting that defaults to 365 days, and ages count from capture time, not import time. This window is separate from the Old badge's threshold (CAL-FR-15). Darks and bias have separate checks: a dark's are exposure, gain, offset, binning, readout mode and temperature within tolerance. Drift past the warning level makes the match a suggestion. A dark or bias outside the window, before or after the lights, is never pinned automatically: it is only suggested, with both capture dates shown, and needs confirmation. This also applies when calibration arrives later (LIB-FR-25). Provisional: a bias's checks are a dark's without exposure. (D-W103, D-W104, D-W114)
- **CAL-FR-22**: A camera with "Don't use darks" or "Don't use bias" (PLAN-EQ-FR-08) gets no dark or bias match: its sessions are settled as No darks or No bias with no warning, and a per-session override pins one. (D-W104)
- **CAL-FR-23**: Calibration doesn't need to be the same across a Project. Its sessions may pin different versions, and the View layout keeps each with the lights that use it (PREP-FR-16). Dark flats are never matched, suggested or assigned. (D-W93, D-W99, D-W101)
- **CAL-FR-24**: The matching thresholds have their own page, Matching, in the Library group of Settings (`/settings/matching`), next to Equipment. It shows them as one table of Setting, Value and Unit, grouped Framing, Flats, Darks and Bias, with one Edit…. Framing holds the framing limits (LIB-FR-04, PRJ-FR-31) and the mosaic panel overlap (PRJ-FR-29), Flats the rotation thresholds and window of CAL-FR-20, and Darks and Bias the windows and checks of CAL-FR-21. Every threshold this contract calls adjustable in Settings is set here, with the defaults of D-W114. Changing the centre or rotation limit applies to later imports only; it re-splits no existing session (D-W107; owner's answer, 2026-10-20). (D-W107, D-W114, D-W148)
- **CAL-FR-25**: The ranking of CAL-FR-20 and CAL-FR-21 also decides whether a calibration in the library is a better match for a session that already has a pin: only a strictly higher rank is, and a tie isn't. This holds however the calibration reached the library: imported, adopted (CAL-FR-07) or restored from hidden (CAL-FR-16). A better match is offered and hinted as LIB-FR-29 says. It never replaces a pin by itself (LIB-FR-25), and its hint is no Update notice (D-W103). (D-W103, D-W106, D-W165)

### Key Entities

Use the [root vocabulary](../063-clean-rebuild-contract/spec.md#key-entities). Feature-specific evidence and decisions retain their input identity, scope, and revision.

- **Calibration assignment**: the input pinned or suggested for one kind of calibration on one light session, in state Pinned, Suggested, No darks, No flats or No bias; for a master it names the version (D-W101, D-W105).
- **Library master**: one record per kind, camera and matched settings, with dated versions; every version keeps its file, SHA-256 and provenance, and none is marked Latest (D-W82, D-W103).
- **Calibration diagram**: the Calibrate step's read-only view of each group's nights and the calibration they get (D-W101).
